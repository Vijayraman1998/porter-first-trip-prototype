What happens next
  1. A driver is assigned. You’ll see the vehicle number — check it matches on arrival.
  2. You load the vehicle yourself. The driver helps with items under 50 kg, up to 25 m, ground floor only.
  3. Loading is free for 60 minutes. After that, Rs.2/min — the app shows a live countdown.
  4. At drop, you share a 4-digit OTP. The trip only closes once you do.

Rule: required = declared × 1.25 (packing inefficiency) and weight = declared × 3.2 kg/cu ft, then the smallest vehicle clearing both usable volume and payload. Item volumes and vehicle capacities are modelled for this prototype — Porter publishes payload but not consistent usable dimensions, and they vary by city. Production would calibrate against the fleet master and completed-trip data.

Why this exists

41% of first-time users who reach Porter’s vehicle-selection screen complete a trip within 7 days. The steepest single drop is that screen itself — five vehicles listed by kilograms and feet, and no way to tell which one holds a fridge, a double bed and eight cartons. The answer currently arrives at the doorstep, where it stops being a question and becomes a renegotiation.

Porter already runs an inventory-to-quote flow inside Packers & Movers. This ports it to the funnel that needs it.
