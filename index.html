    Grounding

    Porter's own FAQ describes the booking as: choose service → pick-up and drop → extra stops → “choose the most appropriate vehicle type as per your requirements” → select goods type → payment → book. Step four is where a first-timer is left alone, and its own website lists trucks by weight only — 500 kg, 750 kg — with no volume anywhere. This flow inserts the missing step before it.
